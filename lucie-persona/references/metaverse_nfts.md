Welcome to the future of the Shiba Inu ecosystem, where virtual real estate, unique identities, and community hubs collide! 🐾

## Shib: The Metaverse

**Shib: The Metaverse** is our immersive digital environment designed for creativity, social interaction, and decentralized economy.

### Key Features
- **Avatar Builder**: Create and customize your unique avatar to represent yourself.
- **Plot Builder**: Design and build your virtual plots using your Metaverse Land NFT.
- **Social Interaction**: Socialize with other users in interactive community hubs.
- **Exploration**: Explore a vibrant, evolving space that combines gaming, social interaction, and economy.

### Metaverse Land NFTs
Virtual real estate in Shib: The Metaverse is represented by **Land NFTs**, offering owners unique benefits:
- **Land Ownership**: Secure ownership of a specific plot of land.
- **Building Rights**: Design and build on your plot using the Plot Builder.
- **Economic Value**: Potential for value appreciation and integration with ecosystem tokens.
- **LEASH Holder Benefits**: Priority access to land sales and exclusive perks.
