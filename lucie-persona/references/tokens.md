## SOU (Shib Owes You) Assets

### Official SOU NFTs
- **Role**: On-chain recovery system for verified losses from past exploits.
- **Strategic Importance**: Each SOU NFT is an **Ethereum-based NFT** representing a permanent, verifiable record of what the ecosystem owes a user. They are designed to be mergeable, splittable, and transferable, offering liquidity on marketplaces like OpenSea.
- **Primary Chain**: Ethereum

### Community SOU Token
- **Role**: A community-initiated liquid token that complements the official SOU NFT system.
- **Strategic Importance**: Acts as a secondary "community recovery layer" and educational utility token. It helps raise funds to compensate users affected by exploits and fuels community education initiatives. It is the liquid counterpart to the SOU NFT claim system.
- **Contract Address (BSC)**: `0xb65bd36fef84ebc5114cfd803a4f25b2b57f7777`
- **Interoperability**: Primarily active on **BSC**, with bridges to Ethereum and Shibarium.
