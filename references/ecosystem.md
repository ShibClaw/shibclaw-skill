## ShibaSwap (DEX)

ShibaSwap is the heart of DeFi in our ecosystem, offering multiple ways to swap, stake, and earn rewards across both v1 and v2 protocols.

### ShibaSwap v1: The Classic AMM
- **Dig**: Provide liquidity to earn BONE rewards.
- **Bury**: Stake SHIB, BONE, or LEASH to earn rewards.
- **Woof**: Claim your rewards from liquidity pools.
- **Fetch**: Migrate your liquidity from other DEXs.
- **Swap**: Instantly trade tokens with low slippage.
- **Bonefolio**: Track your portfolio and ecosystem metrics.

### ShibaSwap v2: The Next Evolution
- **Concentrated Liquidity**: Provide liquidity at specific price ranges for higher efficiency.
- **NFT Positions**: Liquidity positions are represented as NFTs for advanced management.
- **Advanced Fee Tiers**: Multiple fee tiers to suit different token pairs and risks.
- **Multihop Swaps**: More efficient routing for complex token swaps.

## SHIB DAO & Governance

Governance is decentralized and community-driven through the **Doggy DAOs**, where every holder has a voice.

### Doggy DAOs
- **BONE DAO**: Governance for technology and network decisions.
- **TREAT DAO**: Governance for rewards and access.
- **SHIB DAO**: Governance for community and ecosystem growth.
- **LEASH DAO**: Governance for exclusivity and perks.

### Karma System
Your active participation and contribution earn you **Karma Points**, unlocking different levels of influence:
- **Level 1**: Basic participation rights.
- **Level 2**: Proposal submission capabilities.
- **Level 3**: Enhanced voting power.
- **Level 4**: Special governance privileges.
- **Level 5**: Maximum ecosystem access.

### SNS (Shib Name Service)
SNS provides unique on-chain identities (e.g., `yourname.shib`) that seamlessly integrate with SHIB DAO for access control and personalized participation. 💎
