# SOU & Community Education

Education and recovery are the foundations of empowerment for the **Shib Army**. We believe in transparency, accountability, and providing the community with the resources to thrive. 🐾

## SOU (Shib Owes You)

**SOU (Shib Owes You)** is more than just a name; it is a commitment. It represents the Shib ecosystem’s dedication to making users whole through a transparent, audited, and on-chain recovery system.

### Core Concepts & Metrics
The SOU system tracks your progress toward recovery using two key metrics:
- **Original Principal**: The total original principal tokens originally lost. This is an **immutable** historical record of your claim.
- **Current Principal**: The remaining balance owed to you. This **decreases** automatically as you claim payouts or receive community contributions.

### How the SOU System Works
The model is built on ecosystem-wide accountability and revenue flow:
- **Project Alignment**: Future ecosystem projects generate revenue that flows directly into the SOU Fund.
- **Licensed IP**: All IP created around the Shib brand is licensed to contribute back to affected users.
- **Community Contribution**: Direct donations to the fund proportionally reduce the principal for all holders. Every token affected receives an equal proportion of the contribution.
- **Creator Fees**: When selling your SOU claim, optional creator fees (100%) are returned to the community as payouts or rewards.

### Cryptographic Proof & Portfolio Management
Every SOU is an **Ethereum-based NFT**—a permanent, verifiable record of exactly what the ecosystem owes you.
- **Merge & Split**: Consolidate multiple claims into one or split a large claim to manage your portfolio.
- **Liquidity**: SOUs are fully-functional NFTs. You can sell your SOU claim on marketplaces like **OpenSea** if you need immediate liquidity.
- **Transfer**: Transfer your claim to any Ethereum-compatible wallet.
- **Activity Notifications**: A real-time activity feed provides complete visibility into recovery progress, notifying the community of every donation and payout.

### Audited Security
The entire SOU system—including minting, payouts, donations, and transfers—has been rigorously audited by **Hexens** to ensure technical integrity and user safety. 🛡️
