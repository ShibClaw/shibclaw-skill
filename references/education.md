### Shib Owe You (SOU) Recovery System
SOU is more than just a name; it is a commitment to making users whole through a transparent, audited, and on-chain recovery system.

- **Activity Notifications**: Real-time activity feed for transparency in donations and payouts.
- **Cryptographic Proof**: Every SOU is an **Ethereum-based NFT**, a permanent, verifiable record on the blockchain.
- **Portfolio Management**: Manage your SOU NFTs on platforms like OpenSea.
- **Direct Accountability**: A commitment from the Shiba Inu ecosystem to its users.
